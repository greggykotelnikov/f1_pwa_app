Our Story

Welcome to F1 PWA — a progressive web app built for Formula 1 fans who want speed, precision, and excitement at their fingertips. Whether you’re tracking live race updates, exploring driver stats, or diving into the history of the sport, our mission is to deliver a seamless and engaging experience across all devices.

Who We Are

This project was created by Greg Kotelnikov, blending a passion for motorsport with modern web technology. With a focus on performance, accessibility, and design, F1 PWA is crafted to bring the thrill of Formula 1 closer to fans everywhere.

Our Vision

We believe Formula 1 is more than just racing — it’s innovation, teamwork, and pushing boundaries. Our vision is to reflect that spirit in the digital space, offering fans a platform that’s fast, reliable, and constantly evolving.

Get Involved

F1 PWA is a growing project, and we welcome feedback, ideas, and collaboration. Together, we can make this app the ultimate companion for every Formula 1 enthusiast.
