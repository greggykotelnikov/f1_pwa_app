Season Highlights

  • Record-breaking wins by McLaren: Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris delivered multiple victories, including dominant performances in the Qatar Sprint and Australian Grand Prix, keeping McLaren in contention for the Constructors' Championship.
  • New circuits added in Asia: The 2025 calendar expanded with fresh venues in Asia, enhancing global reach and fan engagement, while maintaining classics like Suzuka and Singapore.
  • Major rule changes for qualifying: FIA introduced revised qualifying formats to increase unpredictability, with stricter parc fermé rules and plank wear inspections leading to high-profile disqualifications, notably affecting Norris and Piastri in Las Vegas.
  • Las Vegas drama: Max Verstappen claimed victory under the neon lights, while McLaren's double disqualification reshaped the title fight.
  • Australian Grand Prix opener: Melbourne returned as the season opener for the first time since 2019, marking a symbolic reset and welcoming six rookies to the grid.
  • Ferrari resurgence: Lewis Hamilton's debut season with Ferrari brought renewed energy to the Scuderia, with podium finishes sparking optimism for a new era.
  • Qatar showdown: Verstappen's decisive win at Lusail set up a thrilling finale, while Piastri's sprint victory showcased McLaren's pace.
  • Technical innovations: Teams adapted to evolving aero regulations, with Red Bull and Mercedes introducing mid-season upgrades that shifted competitive balance.
  • Global fan engagement: Enhanced digital coverage and interactive race experiences brought millions closer to the action, with record streaming numbers across platforms.

Championship Standings

Lando Norris (McLaren) leads the championship with 408 points, closely followed by Max Verstappen (Red Bull) on 396 points, and Oscar Piastri (McLaren) on 392 points.

Norris, Piastri, and Verstappen each have 7 wins this season, making it one of the most competitive title fights in years. Norris tops the podium count with 17 podiums, while Piastri has 15 and Verstappen 14.
