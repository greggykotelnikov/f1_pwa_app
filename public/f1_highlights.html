Formula 1 Fun Facts

Swipe through amazing F1 moments and facts!

Formula 1: The fastest racing series in the world.
Drivers experience forces up to 6G in corners.
Teams spend hundreds of millions perfecting performance.
Monaco GP: the most iconic street race.
Pit stops can be as fast as 1.8 seconds.
Aerodynamics determines nearly all lap-time advantage.
DRS gives drivers extra top speed to overtake.
Engines produce around 1000 horsepower.
Each F1 weekend consists of practice, qualifying, and the race.
Lewis Hamilton holds the record for most wins.
Max Verstappen dominates the modern era.
The Constructor's Championship rewards the best team.
